## Reproducibility and boundaries

The latest completed research package has 95 core checks, a no-upstream clean-directory check, complete JSON/GraphML comparisons, dependency propagation checks, archive integrity and representative supported/adverse archived-source replays. These are software/data and scoped replay checks, not full mathematical or application acceptance. [WP128 closure](reports/reproducibility/20261027-F181-history/closure.json) retains the exact scope; [publication QA](reports/reproducibility/20261027-publication/) records checks for this release batch.

The Linux workflow runs its configured core suite and 13 archived replays. It excludes EXP076 strict Linux portability and the separately pinned optional NumPy experiments. A green workflow does not certify Lean, cross-provider review, human review, hardware effectiveness or completion of phase one. [Platform scope](docs/publication.md#actual-first-ci-and-platform-limit).

The official OpenAI `math/` directory is excluded, as are third-party full texts, credentials, caches and bulk local Git history. The pinned upstream revision and checksum manifests identify original evidence without redeploying it. Numerical data, source snapshots and helper logs are compressed and checked by decompression hashes. Temporary external originals are removed after recording their hashes; failed evidence is retained.
